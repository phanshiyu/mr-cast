# Diagram nodes and focus

A storyboard draws graph nodes and sequence participants as one kind of card, with any Lucide icon, a caption, a change mark, a highlight, a badge and a tone, so a diagram can show people and teams as well as services. Graph scenes add edge direction, style and change, a top-to-bottom layout, labelled groups and flowchart shapes. A long sequence scrolls under its pinned participants. Every diagram's `steps` take `focus` refs, and what a step focuses lights half a second before its words while the rest dims. Storyboards written for 0.10 and earlier, with `kind`, `modified` and the older step keys, render as before.

## Sub-features

- `node-card` draws `icon`, `caption`, `change` (NEW, REMOVED struck and dashed, CHANGED), `highlight` (a white glow, no word), `badge` (a pill in `tone`, after any change badge) and `tone` on graph nodes and sequence participants, and `icon` and `tone` on flow actors.
- `marks` draws `change`, `highlight` and `badge` on sequence messages, er tables, fields and relations, states and transitions, and `highlight` and `badge` on timeline events and spans, `badge` on table rows, files entries and test cases, in one vocabulary (NEW, CHANGED, REMOVED). `validate` warns about an over-marked diagram, an added or removed name the diff never mentions, a change mark in an explanation without `explainsChange`, and a change word used as a badge.
- `graph-edges` draws `direction` (`forward`, `both`, `none`), `style` (`flow`, `solid`, `dashed`) and `change` (green, dashed red, accent) on graph edges, with a change legend when an edge changes.
- `graph-layout` lays out `"layout": "TB"` top to bottom, each row of nodes only as tall as its tallest shape, and widens a flowchart's shapes when that lowers its diamonds, so a flowchart draws as large as the frame's height allows (the gallery's at 0.84 scale, 0.65 in 0.14), and draws nodes sharing a `group` inside one labelled box, in bands that never overlap. A storyboard that says `arch` renders as a graph.
- `focus-steps` lights each step's refs in graph (node, `"a->b"`, `"a-b"`, `"group:Name"`), sequence, er (`"entity.field"`, relations), state (`"a->b"`, labels), table (`"col:H"`, `"H@row"`), chart (`"series:s"`, `"s@label"`), timeline (`"span:label"`), files, tests (`"gap:N"`) and intent scenes, keeping an edge's endpoints readable.
- `focus-validate` rejects unknown icons, older icon aliases, unknown node ids, groups and refs, each with the closest names, and warns about groups spelled two ways, self-edges and a step that repeats the one before.
- `pen-marks` draws a step's `mark` (`circle`, `underline`, `box`, `arrow`, `strike`, `cross`) by hand on its first focus ref, or on a code step's lines or `token`, with a handwritten `note` placed off the target. The pen starts only once the step's items are at rest (the camera has stopped, the step's messages or events are drawn and lit, `STEP_SETTLE_FRAMES` after the step starts), completes one frame before the step's voice, and fades when the next step starts. sync-check prints each marked step's `pen` time and `moved`, the share of the frame outside the ink that changed between the frame before the pen and the frame before the voice; it stays under 0.1% (only glow pulses). `validate` checks the values, the note length and the token, and warns past 2 marked steps a scene; `outline` counts marks.
- `graph-shapes` draws `shape` `decision` (a diamond, edges meeting its points), `terminal` (a pill) and `step` (a plain box) with their text fitted inside, lays a graph with a decision out top to bottom with still arrows, and heads it Flowchart. Focus, pen marks, badges, change and highlight work on every shape. `validate` warns about an icon or caption a shape does not draw, a decision with fewer than two branches, and an unlabelled branch.
- `sequence-scroll` keeps a long sequence's rows readable and scrolls them under the pinned participants: each step eases the camera to its messages, a sequence without steps pans first to last during its narration, and a still shows where the last step leaves it. Pen marks on messages ride with the camera. `validate` warns past 24 messages.
- `compare` draws two side frames with their titles and every row label first, then each row at one height on both sides: an arrow drawn left to right between the sides where the cells differ, a dim `=` where they are the same, a code block for a `{ code, lang }` cell (two snippets in one row share a size), and a green check, red cross or amber warning for a `verdict`. Steps show rows as they reach them and light a row, a side (`"side:After"`) or a cell (`"2@After"`). `validate` checks two cells a row, verdicts and side titles, warns past 6 rows, on half-labelled rows and on a row no step names, and fails a cell that does not fit.
- `terminal` draws a window with a title bar (`window`) and plays each entry: the command types at about 40 characters a second after its prompt, the output prints line by line, an `exit` shows as a pill, red with the entry tinted when not 0. Lines keep their indent and wrap, mid-word if they must; the text is 28, 26 or 24px and a longer session scrolls, keeping the newest line at the bottom and scrolling back to an earlier entry or line (`"1:3"`) a step names. A step with a pen mark types within the settle time, so the pen draws on a finished screen. `validate` checks entries and one-line commands, reports output markup errors, and asks for steps past 3 entries.
- `legacy-storyboards` renders 0.10 storyboards (`kind`, `modified`, `messages`, `events`, `entity`, `state`, `transition`, `label`, `row`, `path`) the same as 0.10 did.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Write node fields and `steps` with `focus` in `storyboard.json`, by hand or through the installed skills, then run `mr-cast validate`, `still`, `render` or `play`.
- Read `examples/people/` for a storyboard with no code, and `examples/gallery/` and `examples/demo/` for cell, series, value, transition and field focus.

## Gotchas

- A still is a scene's settled end, which is its last step. To see each step, render and use sync-check's step frames.
- Validation checks icon names against Lucide's own list, which `lucide-react/dynamicIconImports.mjs` provides. An alias such as `home` is rejected because the player resolves icons by their own names only.
- A participant without `icon` has no icon tile, and one without `caption` has no caption line; 0.10 showed a box icon and "participant" there.
- Timeline steps dim the events and spans they do not focus, which 0.10 did not.
- In an explanation an older `modified: true` on a node or message becomes a highlight (no badge), in a diff walkthrough a CHANGED mark.
