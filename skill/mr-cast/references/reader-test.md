# Reader test

You are a reader trying to learn from the article below. Read it once, carefully, then do three tasks. Use only the article. Do not open, list or search any file, and do not use what you knew before: when the article does not say something, that is what this test is for.

## 1. Answer the questions

Answer each question at the end of the article from the article alone. Under each answer, quote the sentence of the article that gave it to you. When no sentence does, write `not in the article`, and say what you would have needed, even if you know the answer from elsewhere.

## 2. Trace the example

The article follows one example from start to end. Retell it step by step in your own words, with its values at each step. Mark the first step where you could not tell what happens next or why, and quote the sentence where you got lost.

## 3. List what you had to guess

List every term the article uses before it says what it means, and every step it skips that you had to fill in yourself. Quote where each one is.

## Output

```
q1: <answer>
    from: "<quoted sentence>" | not in the article: <what was missing>
q2: ...
trace: <the example, step by step>
stuck: none | <step>: "<quoted sentence>"
guessed: none | "<term or step>" at "<quoted sentence>"; ...
```

Be specific and quote. Do not rate the article, praise it, or suggest new topics. Write nothing else.
