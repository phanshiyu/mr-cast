# A short overview for the team (`--audience overview`)

The viewer wants to know what changed or how the parts fit, and why, not how each line works.

- Keep the intent scene, the diagrams, and for the branch the tests scene.
- Show at most 3 `code` scenes, on the lines that carry the main behavior. The source's reference says how to record the rest.
- Say behavior in the diagram narration, with real values, instead of in code scenes.
- Aim for under 3 minutes.

The article stays short too: the problem, the approach, what changes for users, the risk, how well it is tested, and who should look closer.
